## Notifications
<!-- spec: Notifications, Inbox, Web push, Timing, Not only GitHub's schedule, Admin → System → Push, Settings → Notifications -->
- **The bell** (פעמון, top of the screen) is your inbox (התראות): price drops and targets, deliveries arriving today,
  someone shopping now and what they bought, what others added in a shared space, the budget at 80 % and 100 %, and the
  Thursday summary of your week. Computer: it opens under the bell. Phone: a full page — Back or a swipe from the edge
  closes it. Today / Earlier; a dot marks what's new; **Mark all read** (סימון הכול כנקרא); tap a row to open what
  it's about. Phone: swipe a row to the side to delete it. Rows are kept for **30 days**.
- **Received** (הגיע) on a delivery marks it as arrived, right from the notification or the row. **Open** (פתיחה) on
  a price drop opens the item.
- **One switch**: Settings → Account & security → **Notifications** — On for all your devices, or Off (nothing is
  sent; everything still collects in the bell). There are no per-kind settings: Nexus decides what is worth a
  notification and when.
- **When**: everything comes at once — except **Quiet at night** (שקט בלילה): nothing between 22:00 and 07:00, it
  waits until 07:00 (a finished shopping trip at night isn't sent at all), and the week summary, which comes on Thursday
  at the hour you usually open Nexus. A shared space sends the first "Noa added 5 items" at once, then at most one per
  hour (later ones are grouped into the next). One price check sends one notification ("3 price drops").
- **Budget alerts** go to the space owner and the members the owner picks: Space settings → Budget → **Budget alerts go
  to** (viewers can't be picked).
- **Turning them on** on a device: the browser asks once, after you tap **Turn on** (הפעלה) — in Getting started, on
  the card that may appear on Home a few days after "Not now", in the bell, or in Settings → Notifications.
- **Blocked** (חסומות): if you once chose Block, the browser won't ask again. Click the icon next to the address →
  Site settings (phone: Permissions) → Notifications → Allow, then reload. Settings → Notifications shows these steps.
- **iPhone**: notifications work only in the installed app — in Safari tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**, then open
  Nexus from the Home Screen and turn them on there.
- **Not arriving?** (לא מגיעות?) — Settings → Notifications shows this device's state: **Fix** (תיקון) when it is on
  but not registered, and **Test on this device** (בדיקה במכשיר הזה). You never get a notification for what you did
  yourself. Windows: in Chrome, Settings → System → "Continue running background apps when Google Chrome is closed";
  Windows Settings → System → Notifications → Google Chrome on. Android: Chrome (or the Nexus app) notifications on,
  battery Unrestricted. iPhone: only the Nexus app opened from the Home Screen.
- Each device you turn on is listed by browser and system only (e.g. "Chrome · Android"). Signing out stops that
  device's notifications.
