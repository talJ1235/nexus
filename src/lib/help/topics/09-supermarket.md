## The Supermarket tab (סופר)
<!-- spec: Round 18 — Session 1: supermarket data + engines (no new screens), Round 18 — Session 2: the supermarket screens, One supermarket list per space, Sections -->
- The **Supermarket** tab (in the dock, 4th) is the household's grocery list, kept apart from **Products**. It shows
  your store, an estimate against what's left of this month's budget, and the list **in sections** in the order your
  branch is walked (produce first, bread and dairy at the back, frozen last). Rows or tiles: **⋯ → Show as**.
- Tap a row for its sheet: count and weight ("6 · ≈ 1 kg"), a note, a photo of the exact product, section, preferred
  brand and substitutes, one store only, and its history (date, store, price, cheapest).
- Everyone in a shared space sees the same list live. A viewer can look, not change.

## Adding
<!-- spec: Product dictionary + classify, Fast add -->
- Tap **Add an item**: type ("2 חלב 3%", "קילו עגבניות, בערך 6"), say it, or paste a whole list from WhatsApp — you
  see the lines before they're added. Something already on the list grows ("now ×3", with **Undo**) instead of
  appearing twice. On a computer press **/** to add.
- **Scan** a barcode or take a **photo** of the package (the photo counts in the daily AI limit; typing and scanning
  always work). A barcode Nexus doesn't know is named once and remembered for your home.
- A product Nexus can't place lands in **Other** for a moment, then moves to its section by itself.

## How suggestions learn
<!-- spec: Staples + "Build my list" engine, Evening-before draft, Learned walking order -->
- **Nexus suggests** never changes your list: **+** adds, **×** skips this time. Skip something 3 times in a row and
  it comes half as often; 5 times and it waits until you buy it again.
- A product bought on a steady rhythm is suggested when it's due ("every ~9 days"); the rest only in context — before
  Shabbat or a holiday, or "usually with pasta". A new home starts from a basic Israeli basket until about 4 trips.
- **Don't suggest this again** (in the item sheet, or a suggestion's sheet) hides a product for good. Bring it back
  in **Settings → Supermarket → Products you hid → Suggest again**.
- After about 3 trips on the same weekday, one push may come the evening before (19:00–21:00): **"Your list for
  tomorrow is ready"**. It opens the list; it stops by itself after 3 you didn't open. Only the notifications switch
  in **Settings → Notifications** turns it off.
- Nexus learns the order **your branch** is walked in and asks before using it (**⋯ → Section order → Use this
  order**); you can drag sections yourself or reset.

## What Nexus learned
<!-- spec: Trips, Offline + live in the store -->
- **Settings → Supermarket → What Nexus learned** (also in the tab's **⋯**) shows it all: what you told us (✎ opens
  where it's changed) and what it learned — your usual day, other stores, regular products, habits. **✕** forgets one
  thing (Nexus learns it again from what comes next); **Forget everything** clears the learning only — your lists,
  trips and receipts stay.

## Shopping, templates and past trips
- **Start shopping** opens store mode: tap a picture to check it, long-press for not found / took something else /
  price / quantity. It keeps working without signal and sends everything when you're back. What others add while
  you shop appears in place (a push only when the app isn't open).
- **⋯ → Templates & past trips**: Nexus spots routines (the same things on the same weekday, 3 times) and offers to
  keep them as a template **Made by Nexus**, kept up to date until you edit it. Adding a template merges — nothing
  doubles and your quantities stay. Past trips by month: open one to add things again or save it as a template.
- On a computer, **Send to phone** pushes the list to your phone (or a member's) ready to shop.
