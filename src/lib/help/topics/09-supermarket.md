## The Supermarket tab (סופר)
<!-- spec: Round 18 — Session 1: supermarket data + engines (no new screens), Round 18 — Session 2: the supermarket screens, One supermarket list per space, Sections -->
- The **Supermarket** tab (in the dock, 4th) is the household's grocery list, kept apart from **Products**. It shows
  your store, an estimate against what's left of this month's budget, and the list **in sections** in the order your
  branch is walked (produce first, bread and dairy at the back, frozen last). Rows or tiles: **⋯ → Show as**.
- Tap a row (anywhere but its name) for its **product page** when it has its product (see Prices), or its sheet: count and weight ("6 · ≈ 1 kg"), a note, a photo of the exact product,
  section, preferred brand and substitutes ("Usually: Tnuva 3% · 8 of 10 times" once Nexus has seen it), one store only,
  and its history (date, store, price, cheapest). Once Nexus knows your usual brand it shows it as a chip — tap it to
  make it the brand to buy. Tap the **name's letters** to rename it right there.
- **Swipe a row** toward the end of the line to show a grey **Delete**; keep going past about half the row until it turns
  red ("Release to delete") to delete at once — a short or fast swipe never deletes by itself; **Undo** for 5 seconds
  brings it back — or toward the start for a quick **− / +** on the quantity. One row opens at a time; a
  tap anywhere else closes it. On a computer: hover a row for its actions, **Delete** removes the selected row, and
  pasting a list anywhere on the page adds it.
- **Start shopping** opens the start screen in your colour (the palette chip picks a look, per store if you like):
  items, estimate, about how long, the order of the aisles — then **Let's go**.
- Everyone in a shared space sees the same list live. A viewer can look, not change.

## Adding
<!-- spec: Product dictionary + classify, Fast add -->
- Tap **Add an item**: type ("2 חלב 3%", "קילו עגבניות, בערך 6"), say it, or paste a whole list from WhatsApp — you
  see the lines before they're added. Something already on the list grows instead of appearing twice — a cucumber
  added to "1 kg cucumbers" becomes about 9 cucumbers (by its usual weight). A short line says what changed
  ("Cucumbers · now 9", **Undo**). An empty list starts from your templates and what you usually buy. On a computer
  press **/** to add.
- **Voice**: tap the mic and say everything at once ("two milk, bread, half a kilo of tomatoes"). The button becomes a
  level meter with a timer; tap to finish. Nexus understands the recording (one use of the daily AI limit; the audio
  isn't kept) and shows the products to check before they're added. If the mic is blocked, allow it from the lock
  icon next to the address.
- **"+"** on the Supermarket tab opens the camera on the barcode (hold "+" for its menu). Hold the code in the frame:
  when it's read the picture freezes with the code outlined, so you can move the phone away; **Scan another** goes on.
  Tap the screen to focus; if nothing is read after a few seconds, try the light or a little more distance. Switch to
  **Photo**, take it, then **Retake** or **Use photo** — pick **Product** or **Receipt · list** for the frame. Nexus
  says what it sees — a product, a **receipt** (opens the receipt check) or a **shopping list**, handwritten or
  printed (its lines go to the paste preview to check before they're added); switch if it guessed wrong. Something
  that isn't from the supermarket can go to **Products**. A photo counts in the daily AI limit; typing and scanning
  always work. A barcode Nexus doesn't know is named once and remembered for your home.
- A product Nexus can't place lands in **Other** for a moment, then moves to its section by itself.

## How suggestions learn
<!-- spec: Staples + "Build my list" engine, Evening-before draft, Learned walking order -->
- **Nexus suggests** sits under your list, folded to one line — tap it to open (the phone remembers). It never changes
  your list: **+** opens the product with the quantity you usually buy (**Add**), **×** skips this time. Skip something 3 times in a row and
  it comes half as often; 5 times and it waits until you buy it again.
- A product bought on a steady rhythm is suggested when it's due ("every ~9 days"); the rest only in context — before
  Shabbat or a holiday, or "usually with pasta". A new home starts from a basic Israeli basket until about 4 trips.
- **Don't suggest this again** (in the item sheet, or a suggestion's sheet) hides a product for good. Bring it back
  in **Settings → Supermarket → Products you hid → Suggest again**.
- After about 3 trips on the same weekday, one push may come the evening before (19:00–21:00): **"Your list for
  tomorrow is ready"**. It opens the list; it stops by itself after 3 you didn't open. Only the notifications switch
  in **Settings → Notifications** turns it off.
- Nexus learns the order **your branch** is walked in and asks before using it (**⋯ → Section order → Use this
  order**); you can drag sections yourself or reset.

## What Nexus learned
<!-- spec: Trips, Offline + live in the store -->
- **Settings → Supermarket → What Nexus learned** (also in the tab's **⋯**) shows it all: what you told us (✎ opens
  where it's changed) and what it learned — your usual day, other stores, regular products, habits. **✕** forgets one
  thing (Nexus learns it again from what comes next); **Forget everything** clears the learning only — your lists,
  trips and receipts stay.

## Shopping, templates and past trips
- **Start shopping** (it floats above the dock until the end of your list) opens store mode: tap a picture to check
  it, long-press for not found / took something else / price / quantity. A finished aisle stays open — tap its title
  to fold it. Tap a colour on the aisle bar to jump to that aisle. Under a product you'll see the brand your home
  usually buys. **Finish** shows what's in the cart and what stays on the list. It keeps working without signal and sends everything when you're back. What others add while
  you shop appears in place (a push only when the app isn't open).
- Past trips live in **Insights → History → Supermarket** (also **⋯ → Past trips**). **⋯ → Templates**: Nexus spots routines (the same things on the same weekday, 3 times) and offers to
  keep them as a template **Made by Nexus**, kept up to date until you edit it. Adding a template merges — nothing
  doubles and your quantities stay. Saving the list or a trip as a template shows its card first — pictures, name,
  count, estimate and store. Open a past trip to add things again or save it as a template.
- On a computer, **Send to phone** pushes the list to your phone (or a member's) ready to shop.
