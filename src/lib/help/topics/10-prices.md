## Prices, deals and comparing stores
<!-- spec: Round 19 — Tal's fixes after S5 + real products, current prices, deals, store directory -->
- Nexus reads the price files every Israeli food chain publishes by law — the full files every night and the partial
  updates during the day — so the Supermarket list shows **current prices**: each row's price for its count at your
  store, the price per kg / litre / 100 g, and **"updated · HH:MM"** on the basket card (the time of the newest file read
  for your branch). Deals are as the chain published them — **always verify at the till**. If a chain's files stop for
  more than a day the card says **"prices may be old"**.
- **Deals** show under the row in the list's terms: "3 for ₪15 · you have 3 ✓", or "4 for ₪12 · worth taking 4" — tap
  it to set the count. Club deals have their own colour. Nexus also tells you once a day when a product you usually buy
  is on a deal at your store (Settings → Notifications turns pushes off).

## Choosing a product ("Which one?")
- Every list row is a real product (barcode, brand, size) so its price is exact. Added by barcode it's matched exactly;
  typed, Nexus uses what you picked before for that name, your usual brand and the size you typed — "As always · ₪6.80 ·
  Change". When it isn't sure it asks once: **Which one?** shows the candidates with photo, size, price and deals, a
  catalog search, and **Doesn't matter which** (the cheapest of that kind at your store). **Remember** keeps the choice
  for next time; forget it from the product page's "what Nexus learned" switch.
- Rows still without a product say **Choose a product**. Tap any row for its **product page**: the price at your store,
  the same product at every chain, 90 days of prices, cheaper products of the same type; the pencil edits the row.
- Typing in the add bar searches the whole catalog with pictures and your store's price; Enter on free text adds it as
  you typed.

## Comparing stores and the store directory
- **Where is my list cheapest?** (the basket card's line, or Compare): every chain ranked by your list's total with
  deals (club on / off), how much you'd save, which rows a store doesn't have (counted at the average price), and
  **Shop there this time**. It says when splitting between two stores is worth the trip.
- **Choosing your store**: every food chain in Israel with its logo and real branches with addresses (from the chains'
  own files). Search by chain, city or street, or **near me**; each branch shows your list's estimate there. Stores you
  typed before are kept — tap one marked "Choose the exact branch" to link it to the real branch once.
